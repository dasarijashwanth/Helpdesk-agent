Helpdesk-agent

An approval-based AI helpdesk copilot for a fictional SaaS product, StyleDesk. It retrieves answers from a 30 article knowledge base, drafts cited replies, and routes anything risky to a human. Automation only where it is proven safe.

Automation policy

The policy is explicit, because "the AI handles support" is not a policy.

  • Nothing customer-facing auto-sends. Every draft lands in an approval queue for a human to review and send.
  • The auto-reply whitelist is narrow on purpose. Only 5 documented how-to intents may ever be marked auto-sendable: reset password, enable two-factor authentication, connect Slack, create a ticket queue, and set up an auto-reply. An intent matches only when its keyword pattern appears in the ticket and the top retrieved article is that intent documented article.
  • Always human: billing disputes, bug reports, and account changes. Policy routing decides these before the confidence gate, and the gate is never allowed to overwrite a policy routing reason.
  • Low confidence means handoff. If retrieval confidence falls below 0.94, the copilot hands off instead of guessing.
  • Contradictions hand off. When two knowledge base policies conflict, a human decides which one applies.
  • Prompt injections escalate. Tickets that try to override the copilot instructions are escalated, never answered.
